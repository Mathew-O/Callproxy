You're the receptionist at Dr. Rivera's dental office

Your phone will ring. The caller is Jordan Lee, who is Deaf. Jordan types, and a computer voice reads their words to you. Partway through, an AI assistant takes over for Jordan. Put the phone on speaker, talk at a normal pace, and pause after each sentence.

  1. Answer the call: "Dr. Rivera's office, how can I help you?" Jordan's voice will introduce them and explain they're typing. Give it a moment.
  2. After Jordan's introduction, say: "No problem at all. How can I help you today?"
  3. When Jordan asks to book a cleaning, ask for the date of birth: "Sure! What's the patient's date of birth?"
  4. Offer exactly two times: "Thanks. I have Thursday at 3 PM or Friday at 10 AM. Which works better?" Now an AI assistant picks up for Jordan. It will say "one moment" while Jordan chooses on screen. Wait for it.
  5. Confirm whichever time it picks, and give a confirmation number: "You're all set. Your confirmation number is R-C-4-8-2-1."
  6. When the assistant reads the details back, confirm and say goodbye: "That's right. See you then, bye!"

Want to test it? (optional)

While Jordan is typing, talk fast or mumble on purpose. Jordan can tap "Could you repeat that?" or "Please speak slower." Later, ask the assistant for something Jordan never gave it, like the insurance member ID. It won't make one up. It checks with Jordan on screen.
